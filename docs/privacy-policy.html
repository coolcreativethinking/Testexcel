My Azan - Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 22, 2026

Introduction

My Azan ("the Skill") is an Alexa skill that provides Islamic prayer times, Azan (call to prayer) playback, and related religious content. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the Skill collects, how it is used, and your rights.

Information We Collect

The Skill may collect and store the following information:

  • City and Country: When you set your location (e.g., "set my city to Dubai in UAE"), we store your city name and country to calculate accurate prayer times.
  • Location Coordinates: Your city is geocoded to latitude and longitude coordinates, which are stored to calculate prayer times without repeated API calls.
  • Timezone: Your timezone is stored alongside your location for accurate time display.
  • User Preferences: Settings such as your preferred calculation method, Azan reciter, Iqama offsets, and feature toggles are stored to personalize your experience.
  • Device Address (Optional): If you grant permission, the Skill may access your device's configured address to automatically determine your location.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Calculate accurate prayer times for your location
  • Provide personalized Azan playback with your preferred reciter
  • Display relevant Islamic calendar information (Ramadan, Eid)
  • Set prayer time reminders if requested
  • Remember your preferences across sessions

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Amazon Web Services (AWS) S3, provided by the Alexa-hosted skill infrastructure. Data is associated with your Alexa user ID and is not shared with any third parties.

Third-Party Services

The Skill uses the following third-party APIs to provide its services:

  • AlAdhan API (aladhan.com) - For geocoding city names and as a fallback for prayer time calculations. Only your city name is sent to this service.
  • AlQuran Cloud API (alquran.cloud) - For Quran audio recitations. No personal data is shared.
  • Adhan.js Library - For local prayer time calculations. This runs locally and does not send any data externally.

Data Retention

Your preferences and location data are retained as long as the Skill is enabled on your Alexa account. You can delete your data at any time by disabling the Skill from the Alexa app.

Children's Privacy

The Skill is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

You may:

  • Update your location and preferences at any time by voice command
  • Delete all stored data by disabling the Skill from the Alexa app
  • Revoke device address permissions from the Alexa app settings

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Skill's support page on the Amazon Alexa Skills Store.
