Description of Service

My Azan is a free Alexa skill that provides:

  • Islamic prayer times based on your location
  • Azan (call to prayer) audio playback from multiple reciters
  • Quran audio recitations
  • Islamic calendar information (Hijri date, Ramadan, Eid)
  • Eid Takbeer and Friday Khutbah audio
  • Prayer time reminders
  • Iqama countdown timers

Prayer Time Accuracy

Prayer times are calculated using established Islamic calculation methods (ISNA, Muslim World League, Umm Al-Qura, etc.) through the adhan.js library and AlAdhan API. While we strive for accuracy, prayer times may vary slightly from your local mosque or Islamic authority. We recommend verifying prayer times with your local mosque, especially for Fajr and Isha prayers at extreme latitudes.

Islamic Calendar

Hijri calendar dates, including Ramadan and Eid dates, are calculated algorithmically and may differ from dates announced by local moon-sighting committees. Always follow your local Islamic authority for official dates.
